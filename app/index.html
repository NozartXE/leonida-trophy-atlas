    Roadmap to the platinum — seven stages, in order

    Built from the official descriptions and grouped by pacing: story first (it unlocks the world and holds the one missable), side strands and story moments second, the collectible sweep and activity rounds next, robberies and money after that, and the final grind last.

    Missables — the honest case

    One trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides we cite. Everything else is, on current evidence, earnable whenever you get to it — and seven trophies are not known well enough yet for anyone to certify them either way. This page argues each part of that, with sources, instead of just asserting it.

    The one flagged trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

    What it asks: meditate at every meditation spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, for both Jason and Lucia.

    Why it can be missed: the first spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 — meditate at Jason's house, the balcony spot tied to the Down to Earth moment — and it becomes unavailable once Chapter 1 ends. There is no mission replay, so a missed Chapter 1 spot cannot be gone back for on the same save.

    The point of no return: the end of Chapter 1. Before you finish it, meditate at the home spot, and keep a manual save made at the start of Chapter 1 so the chapter can be restarted if the spot was skipped. A manual save at the start of every later chapter is the same insurance, cheaply bought.

    Who flags it: PowerPyx (powerpyx.com) and GTABase (gtabase.com) both name Positive Mental Attitude as the only possibly-missable trophy — read 19 Nov 2026. Note the wording: possibly missable. Neither guide states it as proven.

    Why nothing else is currently considered missable

    • Chapter and mission trophies (Chapters 1–4, the finale, the named story missions) pop from required story beats. Story missions cannot be skipped past, and the game cannot be finished without playing them — so they cannot be walked past by accident.
    • Stranger strands stay available after the story ends, per PowerPyx and GTABase — a strand not started yet is a strand still waiting, not a strand lost.
    • Collectibles and activities (meditation spots beyond the first, WAiNK locations, vehicles, cards, ranges, races, robberies) all remain in the open world after the story, per the same guides. The world does not close behind you; only the Chapter 1 home spot is chapter-locked.
    • The platinum itself only waits for the other 54 — it has no condition of its own that could be missed.

    What we honestly do not know yet

    Seven trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch, per PowerPyx and GTABase: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin and Challenging Tank. For those, "method not yet known" is the truthful label — it is not a certificate that they cannot be missed, and this atlas does not claim otherwise. If one of them turns out to hang on a one-time story moment, its guide here will be corrected.

    PlanetGTA6 (planetgta6.com), cross-checking the official list against Exophase and PSNProfiles, notes that no reliable missable list exists yet — read 19 Nov 2026. The one-flag picture above is the best current evidence, stated with its uncertainty intact — not a settled fact about the game.

    The working rule until the picture settles: meditate at the Chapter 1 home spot before the chapter ends, keep a manual save at every chapter start, and never rely on mission replay — there is none.
