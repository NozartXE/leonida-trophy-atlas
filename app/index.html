    Missables — the honest case

    One trophy is flagged as possibly missable. Two more are not known well enough yet for anyone to certify them either way. Two more are on WATCH: other live entries flag them time-sensitive, and our guides disagree or say nothing — the table shows both sides instead of picking one. Every row links to its guide.

    TrophyVerdictPoint of no returnFlagged by

    The one flagged trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

    What it asks: meditate at every meditation spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, for both Jason and Lucia. Why it can be missed: the first spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and becomes unavailable once Chapter 1 ends. There is no mission replay, so a missed Chapter 1 spot cannot be gone back for on the same save.

    The point of no return: the end of Chapter 1. Before you finish it, meditate at the home spot, and keep a manual save made at the start of Chapter 1 so the chapter can be restarted if the spot was skipped. Who flags it: PowerPyx and GTABase both name Positive Mental Attitude as the only possibly-missable trophy — read 19 Nov 2026. Note the wording: possibly missable. Neither guide states it as proven. One count is disputed: PowerPyx and GTABase count 18 meditation spots; PowerPyx's 100% checklist (15 of 18 meditations) needs only 15 of 18. PowerPyx notes it is unconfirmed whether the Chapter 1 meditation counts toward the trophy — the guide shows both versions, and its counter tracks to 18 so no spot is skipped either way.

    Why nothing else is currently considered missable

    • Chapter and mission trophies pop from required story beats — story missions cannot be skipped past, so they cannot be walked past by accident.
    • Stranger strands, collectibles and activities stay available after the story ends, per PowerPyx and GTABase. One live disagreement: the current #1 entry flags Yago's Race Against Yago (Chapter 3, hourglass icon) as time-sensitive — while our Strangers & Freaks guide says strands stay open. Not settled; race it the moment it appears.
    • A Long Road has no cutoff in our guide — but live entries state 100% completion must finish before the final mission. Treat the finale as the safe deadline: do the count trophies before the last mission, not after.
    • The five side-chain finales (Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident, The Lone Rider, Supply & Demand, Spin On This!) are not watch-listed: PowerPyx notes some quests carry an hourglass icon and fail after a while but are not relevant for trophies, and OutputLag confirms everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales.

    PlanetGTA6 notes that no reliable missable list exists yet — read 19 Nov 2026. The one-flag picture above is the best current evidence, stated with its uncertainty intact — not a settled fact about the game.

    The working rule until the picture settles: meditate at the Chapter 1 home spot before the chapter ends, keep a manual save at every chapter start, and never rely on mission replay — there is none.
